Wind turbine blades are among the most critical and vulnerable components in a wind farm. Subjected to constant aerodynamic loads, lightning strikes, erosion, and environmental wear, blades inevitably require periodic repair throughout their operational life. For most operators, blade repair campaigns are a routine part of maintaining asset performance.
